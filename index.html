READING THIS ATLAS

Two investigations, distinct evidence

Business activity

71 commercial Google Places points with usable opening schedules inside Study_Area_Large. Missing or flagged hours are excluded. Open means scheduled open, including overnight intervals, rather than observed visitors. The schedule collection date is unknown. Weekdays follow calendar days: Sunday 00:00 includes Saturday overnight openings.

Lighting observations

21 supplied building polygons have field brightness ratings from 1 to 5. The researcher confirmed that higher values mean brighter observations. No sampled building has rating 4. The survey date and measurement protocol were not supplied, so these ratings do not establish lux levels or changes over time.

100 supplied pole records include location, owner, recorded bulb type and some inventory dates. UNKNOWN means the bulb field is unspecified, not that the pole is dark. Point halos are a map symbol, not modeled beam coverage.

Overlays and comparison

Open-place heat uses equal-weight Gaussian kernels, with 90 m standard deviation and a fixed scale across times and filters. It is exploratory concentration, not measured footfall or a significance test. Comparing with evening 18:00 shows which scheduled places have closed. Before 06:00, the baseline is 18:00 on the preceding calendar day. Straight-line distance to the nearest mapped pole does not establish sidewalk illumination, route quality or safety.

Geographic reference

Actual OpenStreetMap street and building geometry from a local extract downloaded October 19, 2026. The outline is the larger business study area. Street reference optionally loads OpenStreetMap tiles. The map works with its packaged vector reference without a map API key. Automatic display colors gradually darken from 17:00 to 22:00 and brighten from 05:00 to 08:00. This is a visual time cue, not measured illumination or a seasonal sunrise/sunset calculation. The Light Investigation uses a static night display by default.
